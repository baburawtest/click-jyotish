Diwali, or Deepavali, falls on **8 November 2026** in India. It is a meaningful time to refresh the home, complete pending practical tasks and welcome the festival with a clear mind. Exact puja timing can vary by city and family tradition, so confirm it from a reliable local Panchang before planning a ceremony.

Vastu preparation need not mean expensive renovation or fear-based changes. A thoughtful review of the spaces you use every day is a useful place to begin.

## Start with clearing and cleaning

Give attention to the entrance, living area, kitchen, prayer space and the rooms that your family uses most. Remove broken items, unused packaging, old newspapers and objects that make daily movement difficult. Keep only the changes that are realistic to maintain after the festival.

## Make the entrance welcoming and safe

The entrance is where guests and household members first experience the home. Check practical basics first: clear the path, repair a loose handle or faulty light, tidy shoes and avoid clutter around the door. Rangoli, flowers or diyas can then be added in a way that is safe for children, pets and the building.

## Refresh light and daily-use areas

Replace fused bulbs and clean lamps or light fixtures where possible. A well-lit home is practical as well as festive. In the kitchen, clean frequently used counters and avoid keeping damaged or unusable utensils simply out of habit.

## Create a calm place for prayer or reflection

If your family keeps a mandir or prayer corner, clean it with care and keep it simple. Arrange essential items so they are easy to use. Your family tradition, local customs and the practical layout of your home should guide the details.

## Avoid rushed or unnecessary structural changes

Diwali preparation does not require major demolition, expensive products or changes that create stress. For a home, office, shop or commercial property with a specific layout concern, begin with an individual discussion. A suitable scope can then be decided according to the property and your actual question.

## Write down the one question you want answered

Before seeking Vastu guidance, note the property type, city, the area of concern and what you would like to improve. For example: entrance use, bedroom arrangement, work focus, kitchen layout or a planned move. A simple floor plan or clear sketch can be helpful, but do not send sensitive documents.

## A respectful note

Vastu is spiritual and personal guidance. It does not replace advice from an architect, engineer, electrician, safety professional or legal adviser. Always prioritise building safety and qualified professional advice for structural, electrical or legal matters.
