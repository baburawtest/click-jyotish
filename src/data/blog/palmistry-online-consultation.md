An online Palmistry consultation works best when your question is clear and any photographs are easy to see. The goal is a focused conversation about patterns, strengths and life themes.

## Begin with your question

Choose the one subject you would most like to discuss. It might be a change in career direction, a relationship pattern, personal strengths or a broader question about your next step.

## Use clear, natural light

If photographs of your palms are requested, take them in clear natural light. Keep the camera steady, avoid heavy filters and make sure the entire palm is visible. Both palms may be useful for a complete discussion.

## Wait for the preparation guidance

Do not send pictures to an unverified number or share private documents. After your booking enquiry, Deepak Ji will confirm how and where to send any required images.

## Use the consultation thoughtfully

Palmistry is offered as spiritual and personal guidance. It does not promise a guaranteed result and it does not replace medical, legal, financial or mental-health support.
