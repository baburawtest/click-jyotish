A useful Vastu consultation starts with a clear view of how you use the space. You do not need an elaborate report before your first discussion, but these basics can help.

## Note the property type and main concern

Is it a flat, independent home, office, shop or commercial property? Write down the one or two concerns you want to discuss, such as a bedroom arrangement, home entrance, work focus or a planned move.

## Keep a simple floor plan if available

A basic floor plan is helpful. If you do not have one, a simple hand-drawn sketch showing rooms, entrance and directions can be enough for an initial conversation. The right level of detail will be confirmed personally.

## Observe daily movement through the space

Notice which rooms are used most, where people work, sleep, cook or receive guests. Practical Vastu guidance should work with real life rather than ask for unnecessary disruption.

## Ask before sending photos

If photographs are needed, Deepak Ji will explain what to share. Avoid sending documents that contain bank details, identity numbers or other sensitive information.

## Keep expectations practical

Vastu is personal and spiritual guidance. It does not replace an architect, engineer, structural survey, safety inspection or legal advice. Start with a focused consultation and confirm the suitable scope for your property.
