Karwa Chauth in **Delhi falls on Thursday, 29 October 2026**. It is observed on Krishna Chaturthi, with the vrat traditionally completed after Chandra darshan. For Delhi, the published city-specific Panchang lists the evening puja window as **5:38 PM to 6:56 PM** and Chandrodaya at **8:17 PM**. [Check the Delhi Panchang reference before planning your own observance](https://www.drikpanchang.com/festivals/karwa-chauth/karwa-chauth-date-time.html?geoname-id=1273294), because timings differ by city and family tradition.

This guide is for thoughtful preparation, not rigid rules. Your own family, sampradaya and local Panchang should lead the observance.

## What Karwa Chauth signifies

Karwa Chauth, also called Karva Chauth or Karaka Chaturthi, is a North Indian vrat associated with devotion, prayer and the bond between partners. Many people prepare a karwa, diya, sieve (channi), puja thali and simple offerings. Some families observe the fast together or adapt it according to their health, faith and circumstances.

## A simple preparation checklist

- Confirm the date and city-specific Chandrodaya time two or three days before.
- Keep the puja area clean, safe and easy to use after sunset.
- Arrange the essential items in advance: karwa, diya, wick, oil or ghee, sieve, water and a small thali.
- If you plan to be outdoors for moon sighting, choose a safe place with a clear view of the sky.
- Speak with family members beforehand about the sequence your household follows.

## Keep the evening calm and safe

Choose stable surfaces for diyas, keep them away from curtains and children, and never leave a flame unattended. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, take regular medication or have any concern about fasting, seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Devotion does not require ignoring your wellbeing.

## Why exact local timing matters

The festival follows the lunar calendar, so the date and moonrise are not the same fixed clock time everywhere. A Panchang calculated for Delhi is useful for Delhi; someone in another city should check their own local calculation. This is also why an online calendar can be a starting point, but not the final word for a personal observance.
