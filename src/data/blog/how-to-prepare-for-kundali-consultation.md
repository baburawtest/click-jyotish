A clear question and accurate birth details make a personal Jyotish consultation more useful. You do not need to know every astrological term before your session; a little preparation is enough.

## Keep your birth details ready

For a Janam Kundli reading, keep your date of birth, place of birth and the most accurate time of birth you can find. Check a birth certificate or ask a close family member if needed. If your exact time is unknown, say so honestly rather than guessing.

## Choose one main question

It is natural to have many concerns. Start with the question that matters most right now: career direction, a job change, relationship timing, education, business, or a major decision. A specific question makes a short consultation more focused.

## Share helpful context, not every detail

One or two sentences about your current situation are usually enough. Keep sensitive documents, bank information and passwords private; they are never needed for a Jyotish consultation.

## Keep an open and practical mindset

Vedic Astrology is best used for reflection, timing and personal perspective. It does not remove your responsibility for choices. Take notes during the call and seek qualified professional advice for medical, legal, financial or mental-health matters.

## Book your consultation

When you are ready, submit the private consultation form with your preferred language and time. Deepak Ji will confirm the available slot and next step on WhatsApp.
